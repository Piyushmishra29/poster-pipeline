01WHO WE ARETHE ENERGY
NOT YOUR
AUNTY'S GYM.
Rockwall Fitness is a young, loud, no-excuses gym in Krishnanagar, Bangalore — built for students, lifters and first-timers who'd rather chase a PR than a perfect selfie. The brand is acid-bright, high-contrast and unafraid. It looks like the inside of a good workout: fast, electric, a little chaotic, completely addictive.
BOLD
We shout in lime, never whisper in grey.
FUN
Gym jokes > gym intimidation. We don't take ego, only reps.
REAL
Real members, real sweat, real Bangalore. No stock-photo gloss.

14PHOTOGRAPHYTHE LOOK
DARK. RAW.
RADIOACTIVE.
RAW CAPTURE
→ MONO CRUNCH
→ LIME DUOTONE ✓
Treat every photo: grayscale → high contrast → brightness down → lime overlay at ~30%. The result reads as "Rockwall" even before the logo lands. Shoot real members, real equipment, real sweat.
✓ DO
  • Tight crops on grit — chalk, grips, faces mid-effort
  • Deep shadows, hard light
  • Keep the lime duotone consistent
✗ DON'T
  • Bright, airy, stock-gym smiles
  • Heavy filters or other color casts
  • Competitor branding visible on plates

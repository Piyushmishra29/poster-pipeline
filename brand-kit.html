Rockwall Fitness is a young, loud, no-excuses gym in Krishnanagar, Bangalore — built for students, lifters and first-timers who'd rather chase a PR than a perfect selfie. The brand is acid-bright, high-contrast and unafraid.

04 — ELEMENTS / PHOTO
04.4 — THE LOOK
DARK. RAW.
RADIOACTIVE.
RAW CAPTURE
→ MONO CRUNCH
→ LIME DUOTONE ✓
Treat every photo: grayscale → high contrast → brightness down → lime overlay ~30%. Reads as "Rockwall" before the logo even lands.
✓ DO
  • — Tight crops on grit: chalk, grips, faces mid-effort
  • — Deep shadows, hard light
  • — Consistent lime duotone
✗ DON'T
  • — Bright, airy, stock-gym smiles
  • — Heavy filters or other colour casts
  • — Competitor branding on plates

05 — IN USE / VOICE
05.1 — HOW WE TALK
SHORT. PUNCHY.
A LITTLE SAVAGE.
Like a gym buddy who hypes you up and roasts you in the same breath. Confident, never corporate. Funny, never mean. Bangalore slang sparingly, emoji never.
✓ SOUNDS LIKE US
"Deadlift > Deadline."
"Stop scrolling. Start lifting."
"6 AM. No excuses. See you."
"Books down. Weights up."
✗ NOT US
"Embark on your wellness journey."
"Unlock your fitness potential!"
"State-of-the-art amenities."
"Get fit fast! 💪🔥"
THE FORMULA
PUNCHY VERB + GYM TRUTH + ZERO FLUFF
